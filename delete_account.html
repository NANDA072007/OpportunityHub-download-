OpportunityHub Account Deletion Request

Developer / Publisher: NANDA072007 | OpportunityHub Team

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and immediate. Once your account is deleted, your profile, bookmarked opportunities, resume files, and custom alerts cannot be restored.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

Option A: In-App Account Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the OpportunityHub app on your device and ensure you are logged in.
  2. Navigate to the Profile ("Me") tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap the Notification Settings icon (or Settings menu).
  4. Scroll down to the bottom of the page and tap DELETE ACCOUNT PERMANENTLY.
  5. Confirm your choice by tapping DELETE PERMANENTLY in the warning dialog.

Your account and all associated server/local data will be deleted instantly.

Option B: Web / Email Request (If App is Uninstalled or Inaccessible)

If you no longer have access to the OpportunityHub app, you may submit a manual account deletion request:

  • Email Request: Send an email directly to the developer at nandasunil273@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email address.

Manual web requests are verified and processed within 48 hours.

2. Categories of Data Deleted Permanently

When an account is deleted, the following data categories are permanently removed from our production servers and local storage:

  • Authentication Account: Supabase Auth account ID, login email, password hashes, and OAuth tokens.
  • User Profile Information: Full name, avatar URL, educational branch, degree, graduation year, and career category.
  • Preferences & Settings: Job notification toggles, UI theme choices, location filters, and salary target preferences.
  • Saved Content & Tracking: All bookmarked opportunities and tracked job applications.
  • Custom Alerts & Devices: Saved alert filter rules and FCM device push notification registration tokens.
  • Uploaded Files & Resumes: All resume PDF files stored in private server storage (resumes bucket) and local device resume metadata.
  • Behavioral Logs & Telemetry: Skill proficiency records, interaction history, and recommendation engine signal vectors.
  • Local Cache: All local Hive data boxes stored on your mobile device.

3. Data Retained & Retention Policies

We believe in minimal data retention. The following non-identifiable items are handled as follows:

  • Anonymized Telemetry: Aggregated system delivery metrics are decoupled from your identity by setting the user_id field to NULL. No personal data remains attached to these events.
  • Third-Party Applications: Any job applications or resumes submitted directly to external employer ATS portals via external outbound links are governed by those respective third-party employer policies and are not stored by OpportunityHub.

4. Data Retention Period

Account deletion is executed immediately upon confirmation in the app or within 48 hours for email/web requests. We do not maintain delayed backup retention windows for deleted accounts.
